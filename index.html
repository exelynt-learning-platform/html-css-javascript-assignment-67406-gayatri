Creamy Garlic Pasta

Enjoy this delicious homemade creamy garlic pasta. Made with fresh ingredients, cheese, garlic and cream, this recipe is perfect for lunch or dinner.

★★★★★ 5.0 (Easy Recipe)
⏱️ 20 Min Prep Time
🍳 15 Min Cook Time
🍽️ 2 People Servings

Ingredients

Cooking Instructions

  1. Boil pasta in salted water until it becomes soft.
  2. Heat butter in a pan and add chopped garlic.
  3. Add fresh cream, cheese, salt and black pepper.
  4. Mix the boiled pasta with the creamy sauce.
  5. Cook for another two minutes and serve hot.

💡 Chef's Tip

Add fresh herbs and extra cheese before serving for a richer and more delicious taste.
