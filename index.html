Creamy Garlic Pasta

Enjoy delicious homemade creamy garlic pasta, prepared with fresh ingredients, garlic, cheese and cream. Perfect for lunch or dinner.

★★★★★ 5.0 | Easy Recipe
⏱️ 20 Min Prep Time
🍳 15 Min Cook Time
🍽️ 2 People Servings

Ingredients

Cooking Instructions

  1. Boil pasta in salted water until soft.
  2. Heat butter in a pan and add chopped garlic.
  3. Add fresh cream, cheese, salt and pepper.
  4. Mix the cooked pasta with the creamy sauce.
  5. Cook for two minutes and serve hot.

💡 Chef's Tip

Add fresh herbs and extra cheese before serving for a richer and more delicious taste.
