ITALIAN CUISINE

Creamy Garlic Pasta

A delicious and creamy garlic pasta recipe that is easy to prepare and perfect for dinner.

20 min Prep Time
15 min Cook Time
2 Servings

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 cup fresh cream
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • Salt and black pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil pasta in salted water until cooked.
  2. Melt butter in a pan and add chopped garlic.
  3. Add fresh cream, cheese, salt and pepper.
  4. Mix the cooked pasta with the creamy sauce.
  5. Serve hot and enjoy your delicious pasta!
